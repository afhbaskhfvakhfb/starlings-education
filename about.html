About Starlings Education

Our story, our philosophy, and the heart behind every lesson.

Our Story: From Foundations to Flight

Starlings Education began with a simple belief: that every child is an architect of the future. After years of serving as a teacher and working as the head of Learners' Inn Grammar School, I realized that the world was changing, and education needed to change with it.

We evolved into an online school to remove geographical boundaries and bring a premium, values-driven education directly to your home. Today, we serve a global flock of learners from Grade 1 to Matric/O Levels, providing the stability of a structured school with the flexibility that homeschooling families wish to cherish.

The "Jahan Baan" Philosophy

The Education was named as Starlings under the inspiration of the incredible intelligence and collective spirit of the starling bird. In a specific scenario regarding Pakistani children whether residing in the country or living abroad, we wish to see them growing as Jahan Baan (جہاں بان) — the "Creators and Protectors of a New World."

غرض میں کیا کہوں تجھ سے کہ وہ صحرا نشیں کیا تھے
جہاں گیر و جہاں دار و جہاں بان و جہاں آرا

— Allama Iqbal

Our Vision

To see our students take flight as confident, compassionate, and capable leaders. Whether your child is just starting their journey in Grade 1 or navigating the complexities of Matric/O Levels, they are a "Little Star" in our care, destined to shine bright and protect the future of our world.

A Message from the Founder

"For years, through Learners' Inn Grammar School, my mission has been to provide a space where children don't just learn, but truly thrive. As the world around us evolves, I felt a deep calling to evolve with it—to reach more families and to offer a style of education that meets the needs of the 21st-century learner.

Starlings Education is the realization of that dream.

The transition from a physical school to a digital-first platform isn't just about moving to screens; it's about expanding horizons. I chose the theme of Jahan Baan (Creators and Protectors) because I believe our children from Grade 1 to Matric/O Levels are at the most pivotal stage of their lives. They are not just students; they are the future guardians of our world.

At Starlings, we promise to treat your child's education with the same heart and dedication we've always been known for, now enhanced by the flexibility and innovation of online learning. I invite you to join our 'flock' as we take flight into this exciting new chapter together."

Sadia Shafiq

Founder, Starlings Education
