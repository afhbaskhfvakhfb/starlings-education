Specialised Features

Built Around Every "Little Star"

Great homeschooling isn't just about covering a syllabus — it's about the extra layer of care, skill-building, and flexibility woven around it. These are the programs and structures that distinguish a Starlings education from a standard online class.

From a dedicated Language Lab to a schedule that bends to your family's life across time zones, every feature below exists to support our "Jahan Baan" philosophy of nurturing confident creators and protectors of tomorrow.

🗣️

Language Lab

A dedicated space to master reading, writing, listening, and speaking through interactive tools and live practice sessions — built to make students confident, fluent communicators in both English and Urdu.

🧮

Mental Maths

We move students beyond memorisation, developing sharp mathematical intuition and genuine number confidence through structured mental maths practice woven into the weekly routine.

💻

Digital Skills Sessions

Weekend sessions mastering one practical digital skill at a time — open to every student, including learners who aren't formally enrolled in our full academic program.

💎

Moral & Islamic Values

Universal and Islamic values are woven into every lesson, not taught as a separate subject — building kind, principled hearts alongside sharp, capable minds.

🕐

24/7 Assistance

Questions don't keep office hours, and neither do we. Our team is available around the clock so students and parents anywhere in the world always have support on hand.

📈

Full-Time, Part-Time & Self-Paced Study

Choose full academic supervision, targeted part-time support in a single subject, or a fully self-paced track — move faster where a student excels, slower where they need more time.

🌍

A Global Classroom for Pakistani Families

Purpose-built for Pakistani students at home and abroad to stay connected to their language, culture, and academic roots — while remaining genuinely welcoming to homeschooling families anywhere in the world.

🧑‍🏫

Special 1-to-1 Support

For students who need an individualised, tailored process shaped around their unique needs, interests, and family requirements — learning at a genuinely human pace, guided one-on-one.
