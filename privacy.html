Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your family's information.

Last updated: August 2026

Introduction

Starlings Education ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information of the families we work with. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and how it is used.

Information We Collect

We collect information you choose to share with us, primarily through our Contact Us form. This may include:

  • Your name and your child's grade of interest
  • Email address and phone number (phone number is optional)
  • Any details you include in your message to us

We do not knowingly collect information directly from children. Enquiries and enrollment details are submitted by a parent or guardian.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiries about enrollment, services, or our programs
  • Communicate with you via email, phone, or WhatsApp about matters you've raised
  • Improve our services and website based on the questions families ask us

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

Our Contact Us form is processed through Formspree, a third-party form-handling service, which delivers your submission to us by email. When you contact us via WhatsApp, Instagram, or Facebook, that conversation is also subject to the respective platform's own privacy policy — we encourage you to review those separately.

Data Retention

We retain enquiry information only for as long as needed to respond to you and, where you become an enrolled family, to maintain a record of that relationship. You may request that we delete your information at any time by contacting us directly.

Your Rights

You may ask us at any time to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you and your family. To make such a request, please reach out via the contact details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please reach out via WhatsApp or through our Contact Us page.
